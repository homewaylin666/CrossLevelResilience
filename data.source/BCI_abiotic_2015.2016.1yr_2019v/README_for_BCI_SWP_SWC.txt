BCI_SWP_SWC holds 13 variable:

site		seedling census site (n=200)
x 			x coordinate of the 50-ha plot
y			y coordinate of the 50-ha plot
round		sampling round (Feb, Mar, Apr 2015 or Mar 2016)
date		sampling date
time		sampling time
depth		depth of the soil sample (cm)
swp			soil water potential (MPa)
swc			gravimetric soil water content (%) from fresh mass (f) and dry mass (d), (swc= (f–d)/d)
rain 		1 = sample taken after rain in the sampling round in April 2015
outlier		1 = samples is an outlier, based on a comparison of swp and swc with retention curves constructed for 25 of the seedling sites
exclude		sample was excluded from calculating median SWP per site, because rain = 1 and/or outlier = 1
medianswp 	median swp at the seedling census site
