Analytical strategy

A direct, data-first route to the hypothesis — with no obligatory dependence on the b/r framework (which is kept as an optional extension).

The three moves

  1. Measure resilience at each level. From the BCI census time series, quantify resistance and recovery to ENSO drought separately for individual vital rates, population state variables, and community composition.
  2. Characterise the scaling between levels. Examine how population-level resilience combines into community-level resilience, and what role intraspecific density dependence (conspecific NDD, well documented at BCI) and interspecific interaction coefficients play in that link. This is the core of the chapter.
  3. Probe species-level leverage (exploratory). As one lens — not the spine — ask whether particular species disproportionately shape community resilience. This is your advisor's entry point, now demoted: in a hyperdiverse, high-evenness forest, resilience may turn out to be distributed rather than driven by a few species, which would itself be a result.
Central claim deliberately left open

We do not pre-commit to a specific mechanism (simple aggregation, non-additive transformation/filtering, or species-driven). Which framing the data support is to be decided after exploration — the analysis is built to distinguish them, not to assume one.

One measurement engine across levels

estar (Figueiredo et al. 2025) computes the same family of stability/resilience properties on any state-variable time series — so individual, population and community resilience are measured on a comparable footing, plus a community-matrix route that is exactly the interspecific-interaction link.

The central test, stated plainly

Does variation in functional resilience at the population level, together with the Jacobian (interaction) structure and intraspecific density dependence, relate to community-level resilience — and how? Whether that relation is simple aggregation, a non-additive transformation, or species-driven is left for the data to decide. See the design.
