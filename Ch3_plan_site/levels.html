Transitions — the two places the hypothesis can succeed or fail

j1 · Individual → Population

Emergent term: recruitment & conspecific density — not readable from any single individual.

Mechanism that lives here: conspecific negative density dependence (Comita et al. 2010; Zhu et al. 2015, 2018) and demographic buffering (Santos et al. 2026).

What we estimate: how individual responses aggregate into population resilience, modulated by intraspecific density.

j2 · Population → Community

Emergent term: interspecific interaction coefficients — the community / Jacobian matrix.

Mechanism that lives here: interspecific interactions & stage-specific couplings (Giménez-Romero et al. 2025).

What we estimate: how population resiliences combine through the interaction matrix into community resilience.

Why "transition", not "level"?

In the data, the population is not a separate file — it is the individual records aggregated by species, plus an emergent term (recruitment, density) that no individual carries. So j1 and j2 are aggregation operators, and the difference they create is the cross-scale phenomenon the chapter quantifies.
