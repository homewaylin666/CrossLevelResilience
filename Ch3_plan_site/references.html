How the key references scaffold the chapter

The seven papers in key.ref/ divide cleanly into a conceptual backbone, a measurement engine, mechanism papers, and framing.

ReferenceWhat it providesRole in this study
Falk et al. 2019
Front. Ecol. Evol.
Scaling Ecological Resilience		 Decomposes resilience across space, time & levels of organisation into persistence, recovery, reorganization. Conceptual backbone & vocabulary — the published precedent for "resilience decomposed across levels"; the frame the chapter operationalises and advances with individual-resolution data.
Figueiredo et al. 2025
Methods Ecol. Evol.
estar R package		 11 stability properties; functional (any level) + Jacobian (community matrix). The measurement engine — computes resilience identically at each level; its Jacobian metrics operationalise the interspecific-interaction route.
Giménez-Romero et al. 2025
Ecol. Lett.
Population structure & community stability		 Stage structure & stage-specific interactions strongly shape community stability. Justifies carrying life-stage structure (seedling→tree) and stage-specific density dependence into the population→community scaling; supports principle 2.
Jops et al. 2025
PNAS
Life history drives abundance fluctuations		 Mean-field model: life history predicts abundance fluctuations; 90 species, BCI 50-ha. Same-data template & the open niche — built on this exact data but without disturbance; the chapter adds the resilience / cross-scale-buffering dimension they flagged for future work.
Santos et al. 2026
J. Anim. Ecol.
Demographic buffering, multi-level		 Demographic buffering damps population growth against environmental stochasticity; multi-level view. Names the "buffering source" mechanism in the hypothesis at the population level; method for quantifying vital-rate buffering.
Chesson 2017
PLoS Biol.
AEDT		 Systems track a moving, environmentally-determined trajectory, not a static equilibrium. Defines the reference baseline for measuring resistance/recovery under non-stationary ENSO climate ("recover to what?"); underpins principle 3.
Simmons et al. 2021
Nat. Ecol. Evol.
Stressors by target & scale		 Classify disturbances by the ecological scale they target, not their source. Frames principle 1 (entry level & propagation); aligns the framing with the target journal.
At a glance

Backbone: Falk 2019 · Engine: Figueiredo 2025 (estar) · Mechanisms: Santos 2026 (buffering), Giménez-Romero 2025 (structure), Jops 2025 (life history) · Baseline: Chesson 2017 · Framing: Simmons 2021.
